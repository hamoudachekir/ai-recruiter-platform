## Rules

- Never use em dash or en dash characters in body text. Prefer `:`, `,`, `/` or parentheses.
- For each new job: copy a master to `hamouda_chekir_cv_<company>.tex`, adapt, compile a new PDF. Do not overwrite the masters.
