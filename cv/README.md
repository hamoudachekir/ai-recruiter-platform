## Rules

- Never use em dash or en dash characters in body text. Prefer `:`, `,`, `/` or parentheses. Dates: `Feb 2026 to Aug 2026` / `Févr. 2026 à août 2026`.
- Follow the job description language (English JD → English CV, French JD → French CV, Spanish JD → Spanish CV, Italian JD → Italian CV).
- Masters must keep TECHNICAL SKILLS / COMPÉTENCES TECHNIQUES, CERTIFICATIONS and LANGUAGES / LANGUES as real sections. Do not fold them into one compact block.
- For each new job: copy a master into `hamoudachekir_cv.tex`, set `\TargetTitle` and `\TargetKeywords`, reorder or shorten bullets to match the offer, compile, and replace the previous `hamoudachekir_cv.pdf`. Do not keep company-named copies. Do not overwrite the masters. Keep skills, certifications and languages on the tailored CV.
- Do not invent experience, tools, or employers that are not in the master. Do not put back TuniVert or the AML engine unless the user adds them to the master again.
- AWS on the master is Academy Cloud Foundations, not Glue / production data platforms. TensorFlow and PHP are listed on the master and may be used when the job asks for them.
